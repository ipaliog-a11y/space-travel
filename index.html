Thousands of instanced stars with parallax scrolling and anti-whiteout technology

⚡ Hyperspace Travel

Charge your FSD, jump between systems, and experience stunning tunnel effects

🎮 Flight Assist

🎮 Controls

W S Pitch up/down
A D Yaw left/right
Space Boost (FSD)
0-9 Throttle 0-100%
M Mute audio
H Toggle HUD
R Hyperspace rings
I Invert look
🖱️ Left stick: Steering
🖱️ Right slider: Throttle
🖱️ Drag screen: Look around
⚠️ Browser Requirements: Requires Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, or Edge. WebGL and Web Audio API required. Not supported on Internet Explorer or old Android browsers.
