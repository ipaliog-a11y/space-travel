## Findings

**Prior recs that landed.** Softer well (#1), camera bank (#5), jump-ly weight (#7 at 0.3), regime HUD (#10). Decision #011 matches the code. Good.

**Jump economy is sane.** ₡2,112 funds a Mk I with room; it no longer dwarfs every local row. Keep local ₡220.

**Stick depth up; economy depth flat.** Orbiting with throttle off is possible in code. Merchant still has no spatial arb. Sitting on Watch is still the only "market" play.

**Starter pick helps once.** Choosing Hauler at t=0 opens bulk rows immediately. Scout still has no job kind — surveys exist, paid pathfinder packets do not. Clipper still a label until VIP or sprint jobs.

**Hull treadmill remains.** ₡100k Courier buy ≈ 114 local runs (or ~47 jump packets). No used/NPC bay yet.

**Shared wallet across careers.** v0.1.17 makes slots feel like separate pilots, but ₡ and hulls are still account-wide. That fights the fiction and softens the treadmill (three diaries, one bank).

**Ranks research is ready, not live.** Wiring XP → promotion without a used-hull path at rank 3 just gates a second bay behind grind with nowhere to spend.

## Recommendations for Grok Build

1. **Per-hub price offset on the same tape (repeat).** Stable hub bias ±12–20% from `hash(hub|good)`. Hauling a pad lot becomes a route. Do this before mining.
2. **Put a cost on sitting (repeat).** 2–4% bid/ask and/or pad storage fee per tick. Same-quote churn is zero-skill.
3. **Mass in well/free thrust (repeat).** Well: `acc = drive * 2.2 / max(0.45, def.mass)`. Free lerp: `3.1 / mass`. Do not retune turnRate in the same pass.
4. **Scout / Clipper job kinds (repeat, still open).** Courier-sized survey / nav-film packets (qty 2–6) that prefer Scout (`surveySec`) and Clipper (in-system OD). Starter Scout needs a paid loop, not only a free hull.
5. **Used / NPC bay at rank 3 (repeat).** Worn Courier ~₡40–70k when the second bay unlocks. Do not cut local ₡220. Pair with wiring `ranks.ts` XP → promotion if you turn the ladder on.
6. **Ship HUD Park lamp.** BUILD already notes it. Lock-line text is easy to miss; lamp on `regime === "park"` without requiring look-at-planet.
7. **Gate / Pilot copy: say the wallet is shared, or split credits per slot.** Three careers + one bank is confusing. Prefer per-slot credits if careers are meant to be separate playthroughs.
8. **Playtest checklist for #011 (no code).** Arrive Helios I parks. Throttle off in well circles. OD punches out. Dock corridor unchanged. Q/E banks and recenters. Confirm before touching damp again.
9. **Do not start mining / fleet until (1) lands.** An Extractor on a flat tape is another wait-for-tick machine.
10. **Leave jump-ly at 0.3** unless playtest shows jump spam again; then add interdiction (BUILD 11) before nerfing further.
