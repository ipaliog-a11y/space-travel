## Findings

**Mining has decisions at the stick, none at the market.** Survey, park vs band, Extractor vs sip, which wild world — good. Selling is dump-on-Watch. Without hub bias or local surplus, harvested goods are another Wait-for-tick lot.

**Extractor is correctly the speed hull, not a bigger bite.** `extractQty = mining` grade; tests lock Extractor ≤3s and Clipper ≥12s. Keep that split.

**₡0 cost + flat tape = AFK money after unlock.** 36s ore fill → ₡448 with no fuel spent on the pull itself (travel/refuel still apply). Helium-3 worlds are the obvious spam target. Prior rec #9 said do not start mining until hub bias; that ship sailed — fix the tape now.

**Sip hulls are good onboarding, bad long-term.** Courier can discover mining without buying Extractor; once Extractor exists, sip rates are flavor only. Fine for v1.

**Hull treadmill worse.** Extractor at ₡160k sits between Tug (₡130k) and Tender (₡180k) with no used/NPC path. Mining income accelerates the buy once you already grind jobs — then mining outpaces jobs on high-base goods.

**Scout still unpaid pathfinder.** Surveys gate mining, but Scout has no paid survey/nav packet kind. Starter Scout still leans on Watch or sip mining.

**Cosmetic traffic is fine.** Do not pay it until fleet contracts (BUILD 10).

**Helion + Park lamp:** prior #6 done. Leave alone.

## Recommendations for Grok Build

1. **Per-hub price offset on the same tape (critical, repeat).** Stable bias ±12–20% from `hash(hub|good)`. Mined lots and pad hauls become routes. Do this before retuning `extractSec` or adding extractor pads / local surplus.
2. **Sitting cost (repeat).** 2–4% bid/ask and/or pad storage fee per tick. Stops same-quote churn and "mine → sell here forever" when bias is small.
3. **Local surplus on the harvest hub (only after #1).** When selling goods that match the world's yield at the nearest lock, apply an extra −8–15% so the profitable move is haul-out, not dump-at-first-dock. Spec already hinted this; code left it for later.
4. **Soft fuel/wear tax on Extract / Scoop cycles.** e.g. 0.4–0.8 T1 per successful pull (Extractor higher sip rate). Makes park-and-print cost something without nerfing qty.
5. **Cap AFK print without killing discovery:** optional soft cooldown or diminishing grade after N consecutive pulls on the same body until you leave SOI. Prefer #1+#4 first.
6. **Scout survey / nav-film job kind (repeat).** Qty 2–6, prefer low `surveySec`. Starter Scout needs a paid loop that is not only mining sip.
7. **Used / NPC bay at rank 3 (repeat).** Worn Extractor or Courier ~₡40–70k when second bay unlocks. Do not cut local ₡220.
8. **Mass in well/free thrust (repeat).** `acc = drive * 2.2 / max(0.45, def.mass)` in well; free lerp `3.1 / mass`. Extractor mass 1.38 should feel heavier than Courier 0.7 when pulling.
9. **Playtest checklist (no code).** Grade-2 rocky Extractor fill→sell ore. Scoop Helium-3 on a gas giant in bands (not well). Sip with Courier once. Confirm Watch price identical across hubs today. After #1, confirm haul-out beats dump-local on surplus worlds.
10. **Leave Extractor catalog at ₡160k and `extractSec` 2.6** until #1–#2 land; then retune if helium-3 dumps still beat jump packets per minute.
