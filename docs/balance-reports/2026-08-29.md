## Verdict

Jobs are not a treadmill: a stock Courier lock-to-lock covers repair and T1/T2 with hundreds of ₡ left, and a single jump packet funds Mk I. The grind is the hull catalog (₡100k–₡200k) and Rel/slot alts, farmed by repeating the same two Courier rows on a six-line board. Merchant adds a Watch and pad crates, but one galactic tape plus identical buy/sell means you sit and wait, you do not route. Hulls handle differently (turn, OD, hold, T2) and only Courier/Hauler/Tender/Tug have jobs; Scout/Clipper/Tender-as-tanker/Tug-as-harbour are labels. The well still glues (relative velocity zeroed on coast; park spring on wild worlds); you cannot orbit. A player who likes flying Kepler hops will stay for the view. A player looking for compounding economic decisions will spam Courier → Deliver → Refuel until the tape is a screensaver.

## Live numbers

### Jobs

Pay is `qty × (local AU + jump ly) × kind rate`, clamped ₡1–₡50,000. No ₡1,000 floor. `JUMP_LY_AS_AU = 1`. Same-planet docks floor 0.25 AU.

| Kind | ₡ / u / AU | Qty rolls |
|------|------------|-----------|
| courier | 220 | 2, 3, 4, 6 |
| hauler | 38 | 16, 20, 24, 32 |
| tender | 55 | 10, 12, 14, 16 |
| tug | 70 | 8, 10, 12 |

Worked courier lock-to-lock (4 u × 1 AU): payout **₡880**, wear ~2.3 pts → repair **₡35**, T1 OD ~₡21, leftover **~₡824**. Jump courier 4 u × 8 ly = **₡7,040**.

Starter Courier `cargoCap 8` takes courier listings only. Rank-1 hangar is one bay.

### Merchant

Forty goods, eight per lock, one galaxy tape, tick 15 s, same buy and sell quote. Pad warehouse is per save slot per hub. Because price is galactic and bid = ask, flying cargo does not create arb. Only play is buy, wait for the walk, sell (or park on the pad).

Credits are account-wide; save slots share the wallet.

## Findings

**Jobs vs costs — leftover, not treadmill.** Courier 4 u × 1 AU = ₡880 vs ~₡35 repair + ~₡21 OD T1. Jump packets dwarf local costs.

**Board vs hangar.** Starter Courier can accept courier jobs only. Four of six board rows are dead until a bigger hull is owned.

**Merchant is timing, not routing.** Same ₡ everywhere, same buy and sell. Pads let you park inventory so jobs can use the hold — that is a decision. Flying the lot to another lock is busywork.

**Hulls.** Turn, cruise, OD, cargo, T2, jump range, survey time differ and are felt. `def.mass` is not. Scout/Clipper have no job kind.

**Physics.** Well glue fixed planet-runs-away by welding you to the body. Throttle off = park, not orbit.

**Saves / T2 / pump.** Slots are useful but share credits/hulls. T2 gates jumps. Flat pump prices do not create where-to-fill.

**Engagement.** Hours of flight yes. Hours of loop decisions no — one action (Courier deliver) pays, one action (wait on Watch) speculates, and neither compounds.

## Recommendations for Grok Build

1. **Softer well (PHYSICS_GROK_BUILD slice 1), now that glue exists.** Keep the Kepler-relative frame. Stop zeroing relative velocity on coast; damp at ~`1-exp(-0.35 h)` or lighter. Park spring on Arrive / first SOI capture only, not every frame on wild worlds. Success: throttle off, you circle at constant altitude; Arrive still parks; OD still punches out. Do not touch the 10-bay docking corridor.
2. **Per-hub price offset on the same tape.** Keep one galactic walk as the index. Multiply each listing by a stable hub bias (hash of `hub|good`, ±12–20%) so ice is cheap at an ice world and cores are dear at a yard. Then hauling a pad lot is a route. Do this before mining.
3. **Put a cost on sitting.** Either a 2–4% bid/ask, or a pad storage fee per tick, or both. Instant buy/sell at the same quote is zero-skill churn.
4. **Mass in the well/free thrust terms.** Well cruise: `acc = drive * 2.2 / max(0.45, def.mass)`. Free-space lerp: `3.1 / mass`. Do not retune `turnRate` in the same pass.
5. **Camera bank.** Pass `clamp(bankRoll, -0.35, 0.35)` into `viewFromLook` instead of `0`.
6. **Give Scout and Clipper a reason to leave the hangar before ₡100k.** Courier-sized survey packet / nav film jobs (qty 2–6) that prefer Scout (surveySec) and Clipper (in-system OD).
7. **Retune jump pay or jump wear.** `JUMP_LY_AS_AU = 1` makes 4 u × 8 ly = ₡7,040, ~8× a local run. Drop jump-ly weight to ~0.25–0.35 AU, or add interdiction/risk (BUILD 11) before this is the only rational job.
8. **Hull market is the treadmill — add a used/NPC bay, don't nerf job pay.** A worn NPC Courier at resale (~₡40–70k) at rank 3 (second bay). Do not cut local ₡220.
9. **Split credits per save slot, or say so in Gate copy.** Account-wide ₡ plus three careers is an accidental shared wallet.
10. **Regime HUD.** Surface Free / Well / Park / Od / Dock, and whether speed is relative or inertial.
11. **Watch: show hub bias and your basis on the spark.** When (2) lands, mark this lock vs tape. Until then, hide locks-that-trade-it as a haul suggestion — it implies arb that does not exist.
12. **Do not start mining/fleet until 1 + 2.** An Extractor feeding a flat galactic tape is another wait-for-tick machine.
